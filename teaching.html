Teaching

My teaching philosophy is grounded in evidence-based, inclusive pedagogy that meets students where they are. I rely on active learning strategies, such as think-pair-share exercises and real-time formative assessment, to keep students engaged, and I collect regular feedback through beginning- and mid-quarter surveys so I can adapt my approach to each group's background and needs. Whether leading discussion sections, guiding hands-on observing labs, or delivering guest lectures, I aim to explain concepts clearly and equip students with the tools to work through problems themselves rather than simply handing them answers. I bring that same philosophy to mentorship, working to create structured, supportive spaces where students can grow as researchers, communicators, and future scientists.
